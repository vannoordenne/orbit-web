Meet the founders

Marise van Noordenne & Jolijn Friederichs

Marise van Noordenne
Creative Technologist · Critical AI
marisevannoordenne.nl

Marise van Noordenne is a creative technologist, researcher and educator at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (AUAS). Her background combines Creative Technology (BSc), Media Technology (MSc) and studies in Artificial Intelligence.

Her research explores provocative and speculative design methods for AI literacy and explainability, with a focus on how interactive and experiential prototypes can support ethical reflection and understanding of complex AI systems.

Jolijn Friederichs
Critical Maker · Value Sensitive Design
jolijnfriederichs.com

Jolijn Friederichs is a critical maker, artistic researcher and educator at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (AUAS). She holds degrees in Fashion Design (BSc) and Digital Design (MSc).

Her work investigates how Value Sensitive Design can be integrated into technological making, with a focus on responsible innovation and the ethical implications of hyper-personalisation. She explores how design practices can help students engage with ethical boundaries and systemic consequences in digital technology.

Together, Marise and Jolijn founded the Dark Tech Studio at the ICT programme at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (AUAS), where students explore emerging technologies through critical and speculative design. Additionally, they develop the Dark Tech Method: a transferable approach to critical making in a digital society. Read more about the originating research project Tinker: Ethical Techlab on the CoE-CI website.

Alongside this work at AUAS, they run or.bit as a practice-based research collective. Through or.bit they offer workshops, talks, educational programmes, research collaborations and interactive installations, for universities, public organisations, cultural institutions and industry.
