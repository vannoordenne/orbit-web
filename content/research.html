Research

We investigate the relationship between technology and society. Our research produces publications, frameworks, methods and analyses around emerging technologies: AI, critical technology, speculative design and socio-technical systems.

Current research lines

Investigating how deliberately exaggerating and materialising the dark side of emerging technologies can foster critical discussion, ethical reflection and technological literacy.

Human-AI Interaction

Exploring how people experience, understand and interact with AI systems, with particular attention to critical AI literacy and experiential approaches.

Values in Emerging Technology

Examining how values, power, responsibility and sustainability are embedded in the design and use of emerging technologies.
