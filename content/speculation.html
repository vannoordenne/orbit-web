The Speculation Game

NEW EDITION — INTEREST LIST OPEN

What if you had to build a future out of the cards you'd rather not draw? The Speculation Game is a speculative card game in which players assemble unlikely technological futures and surface the assumptions, values and power structures hidden inside emerging digital systems.

Players combine prompt cards to construct unexpected future worlds, pushing past familiar narratives toward harder questions about the systems that shape digital life. Play sits at the centre: it turns complex technological questions into something people can argue about, negotiate and reflect on together.

We developed the game into the workshop Designing Dark Tech, facilitated in educational and public settings including the Dark Tech Studio at AUAS, the Society 5.0 Festival, AI020, and other courses and events at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences.
