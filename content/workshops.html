📄 Designing Dark Tech ▼

What happens when we stop developing solutions and start designing the systems we fear? In this hands-on workshop, participants explore the shadow sides of emerging technologies by creating provocatypes: speculative and critical prototypes that reveal ethical tensions, systemic bias, and unintended consequences. Using The Speculation Game participants work with thematic Dark Tech cards (such as personas, technologies, and revenue models) to design fictional systems that surface hidden assumptions and power structures. These speculative scenarios spark critical discussion about how technology shapes, and is shaped by, society.
